# NotExtension privacy policy

**Effective:** 12 November 2026

NotExtension lets you highlight text on web pages, pin a highlight with a note, and keep one note per website. This
policy says what it stores, where, and who can see it.

## What NotExtension stores

- **Your highlights:**
  - the text you highlighted, and a few words before and after it, so the highlight can be found again when you come
    back;
  - the address of the page;
  - the colour you chose, the note and tags you added, whether it is pinned, and when you made it.
- **Your site notes:** the text you write for a website, stored under that website's address.
- **Your settings:** theme, autosave, and which features and sites you switched on or off.

NotExtension does not store anything about pages where you make no highlight or note. It does not record which pages
you visit.

## Where it is stored

Everything is stored in your browser on this device (`chrome.storage.local`). NotExtension has no account and no
server. It makes no network requests, sends nothing to the developer or anyone else, and uses no analytics or
advertising. Browser sync does not copy it to your other devices.

## Who can see it

Only you, in your browser. The developer cannot see, collect or receive your data. It is not sold, not shared, and not
used for anything except the features you use.

## Keeping or removing your data

- **Export:** Options › Data & Privacy exports your highlights (JSON or Markdown), notes and settings.
- **Delete one item:** remove a highlight from the page, the popup or the dashboard; delete a site note in the popup.
  Removing the last highlight on a page leaves that page's address behind as an empty entry; it goes when you delete
  everything.
- **Delete everything:** remove the extension from your browser; the browser deletes all of its stored data.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Read and change data on all websites | Highlights are made and shown again on the pages you read, so the extension runs on web pages. It reads only the text you select and the text needed to find your highlights again. |
| Storage | Your highlights, notes and settings are kept in the browser. |
| Context menus | "Pin", "Unpin" and "Remove highlight" in the right-click menu. |

## Chrome Web Store user data policy

NotExtension's use of the information it handles complies with the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies), including
the Limited Use requirements.
